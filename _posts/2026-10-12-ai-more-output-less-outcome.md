AI has increased the amount of communication, in other words output, but not the outcome. It has decreased the amount of ideas overall and the density of notions per communication unit, and increased the cognitive load for each knowledge worker.

As always, there's no silver bullet, however we can work with these issues even now.

## Density of communication

You probably don't want to make AI's text too dense. A high density text can't be easily understood. Try a philosophy book (e.g. Hegel:). If a text is too dense, there's a high chance you'll lose ideas and connections between them.

At the same time, AI's text may look too low on density: you lose the idea while trying to understand it. My solution to it is proper instructions and experimentation. More on that in the next sections.

## Amount of new ideas

Novelty of AI's ideas is an answered question: AI does not produce new ideas yet. An LLM works as a generator based on a huge amount of texts, so it finds something applicable in your context, but doesn't create anything from scratch.

However, novelty is in the context. For one, the idea of Agile is a disruption of their whole identity, but for another the same happens when they hear of the USSR's 5-year plans. So AI can generate something new for your specific context.

Don't expect AI to produce a new market disruption for you, but use it as an extended mind — and the chance to get a valuable result gets higher.

## Amount of output and cognitive overload

I think it is the easiest thing to clean. As we write prompts or set up how Claude answers, we can set the discourse characteristics we'd like to see: 'be strict, don't waffle' and so on.

You can add these instructions in each session, in Claude itself, or make them a part of your Skill.

I also believe that text in the form of a text (just words following each other) is not a cure. You may ask AI for a visualization instead of text, so that all the output is visible at one sight. But I have already spoken about it [here](/2026-10-06-are-acceptance-criteria-a-section-of-a-backlog-item.html) and [here](https://www.certificate.tips/blog/business-analysis-diagrams-case-study).

So use your AI buddy with the proper density in the text, don't expect it to create a new business for you, and set up proper rules.
