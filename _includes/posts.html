{%- comment -%} The posts on the home page (the five latest, include.limit=5, without the heading Blog and the filter) and on the Blog page (all, with a filter by keyword, that is a post's tag, and by language, see assets/js/blog-filters.js). Several keywords and several languages can be chosen: a post is shown if it has one of the chosen keywords and is in one of the chosen languages. The options start with a small heading Tags, the languages are under the keywords, in the same options, with a small heading Languages; when all posts are in one language, they are not shown. On wide screens the filter stands right of the list like the keywords in a post, in the same place as there: a click on the name shows the options under it. On narrow screens it is under the title: a click on the name opens a menu with the options. On phones the name is the cabinet alone, right of the title, under the menu. The number after the name tells how many options are chosen. On wide screens the filter (named Filters) has the catalogue cabinet from the posts: when its options are open, the cabinet is turned and its drawer is pulled out. Each option has its own fill colour for when it is chosen: data-fill counts the keywords and then the languages in a row (the colours are in assets/css/main.scss). Without the script the filter stays hidden and the whole list is shown. {%- endcomment -%} {%- capture langs %}{% for post in site.posts %}{{ post.lang }}|{% endfor %}{% endcapture %} {%- assign langs = langs | split: "|" | uniq | sort %} {%- comment -%} Tag names in alphabetical order, whatever their case. {%- endcomment -%} {%- capture tags %}{% for tag in site.tags %}{{ tag[0] }}|{% endfor %}{% endcapture %} {%- assign tags = tags | split: "|" | sort_natural %} {%- unless include.limit %}
