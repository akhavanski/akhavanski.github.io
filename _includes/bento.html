The kinds of sushi that come in turn are in the row's data-kinds, data-next is the one to come next; the slots are --step apart. It goes inside a button with the class menu-toggle: hover brings the chopsticks to pick up the last sushi, a click has them carry it away (assets/css/main.scss, assets/js/menu.js). The back chopstick is drawn behind the sushi, the front one over the box; a new sushi slides in from behind the left wall (the clip). {%- endcomment -%}
