{%- comment -%} The posts on the home page (the five latest, include.limit=5, without the heading Blog and the filter) and on the Blog page (all, with a filter, see assets/js/blog-filters.js). The filter has three groups, each with a small heading: Tags (a post's tags), Languages and Published on. Several tags and several languages can be chosen: a post is shown if it has one of the chosen tags and is in one of the chosen languages; the headings say so ("any of them"). Published on has the name of the other blog (or "Other sites" when there are several) and of this one; only one of the two is on at a time, with none on all posts are shown. Languages are shown only when two languages have $min_lang_posts posts or more; until then a post in another language than English has a small mark with its language after its title (lang-badge). Each option shows how many posts it would give with the other groups as they are; an option that would give none is pale and cannot be chosen. A tag can have a hint, shown when pointing at it: _data/tags.yml. The options are always out. On wide screens they stand right of the list, under the name Filters; on narrow screens under the title, one line per group, each scrolling sideways. Under the filter one line says in words what is shown, e.g. "5 posts tagged AI or Product, in English". When no post fits (a link can ask for that), the line offers to take off one of the options, with the number of posts that would bring back. The number after the name tells how many options are chosen. The catalogue cabinet by the name is a toy: pointing at it turns it a little, a click turns it to profile and pulls out its drawer, the next click puts it back; the options stay as they are. The name does not move with it: it stands clear of the pulled-out drawer. Each option has its own fill colour for when it is chosen: data-fill counts the tags, the languages and the two places in a row (the colours are in assets/css/main.scss).
