--- layout: page title: Blog permalink: /blog --- {%- comment -%} All posts with filters by language and by keyword, that is a post's tag (see assets/js/blog-filters.js). Several languages and several keywords can be chosen: a post is shown if it is in one of the chosen languages and has one of the chosen keywords. The names of both filters are in one line; a click on a name opens a menu with its options, the number after the name tells how many are chosen. Each option has its own fill colour for when it is chosen: data-fill counts the options of both filters in a row (the colours are in assets/css/main.scss). Without the script the filters stay hidden and the whole list is shown. {%- endcomment -%} {%- capture langs %}{% for post in site.posts %}{{ post.lang }}|{% endfor %}{% endcapture %} {%- assign langs = langs | split: "|" | uniq | sort %} {%- comment -%} Tag names in alphabetical order, whatever their case. {%- endcomment -%} {%- capture tags %}{% for tag in site.tags %}{{ tag[0] }}|{% endfor %}{% endcapture %} {%- assign tags = tags | split: "|" | sort_natural %}
